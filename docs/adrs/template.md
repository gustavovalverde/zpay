## Context

Problem framing. Workload shape. Candidates evaluated. Constraints that narrow
the choice. Present tense, no past-tense narration of the search.

## Decision

The commitment. Use bold sub-sections when it is a multi-part decision.

## Rationale

Why this option won. Optional; include when the Context does not make the
choice self-evident.

## Consequences

Positive:

- one bullet per material consequence

Negative:

- one bullet per cost the team accepts by choosing this

Neutral:

- facts that are neither pro nor con but are relevant

## Switch Criteria

Optional. Gates for replacing the choice. Each gate is necessary; "all of"
not "any of".

## Alternatives Considered

### Alternative 1 name

One paragraph rejection reason.

### Alternative 2 name

One paragraph rejection reason.

## Out of Scope

Optional. Decisions deliberately deferred or excluded from this ADR.
